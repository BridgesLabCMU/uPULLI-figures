OPENTRONS LIQUID-HANDLING PROTOCOLS

The archive opentrons-protocols.zip contains the protocols used to prepare imaging
plates for this study. Each protocol, and the dataset it produced:


  Tn imaging, wells 1-2.json
      Initial genome-wide imaging of the transposon library (~2,850 mutants).
      Figure 3A; Figures S4A and S5; Data S1.

  Darin plate_ dilution wells1-4.json
      Replicate re-imaging of the mutants selected from that screen
      (158 mutants, 3,669 wells).
      Figures 3B and 3D; Figures S6 and S7; Interactive Plots 1-3.

  Kleb_ Tn-imaging_wells1-2.json
      Klebsiella pneumoniae transposon mutants.
      Figure 5B.

  Sophia_3_column_3_plate_200ul_media_update.py
      Eight-species bacterial panel.
      Figure 5C.


PLATES PREPARED BY HAND

The compound-treatment and clean-deletion plates were pipetted by hand rather than
by robot, so no protocol is included for them:

  260522_hand_cmpdTreatment_1                          Figure 5A
  260521_hand_cleanDeletion_1, 260522_hand_cleanDeletion_2   Figures 4A and 4C

Robot-prepared versions of both experiments were run earlier but are not the data
shown in the paper. The robot_ or hand_ prefix in an experiment identifier records
which preparation was used.
